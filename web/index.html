The cost of the privacy policy

Each point is one setting of the statistical gate, over 500 simulated outbreaks with detection held fixed. Tightening the gate moves first disclosure later and lets more people be infected before anything is said. Infections are the unbiased figure: outbreaks that were never disclosed count their full day-30 total.
