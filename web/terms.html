Terms of Service

Plain terms for the demonstration site.

What this is

This site runs an illustrative simulation of the MicroCluster health-signal system. The population, the outbreak, the reports, and every parameter are synthetic and are chosen to produce a legible demonstration. The simulation does not model, predict, or describe real disease transmission in any real community.

Not medical advice

Nothing on this site is medical, epidemiological, or public-health advice. The system does not diagnose anyone and is not designed to. Do not use any output here to make a decision about your health or anyone else's.

Acceptable use

You may run simulations, read the precomputed results, and inspect the code. Do not attempt to overload the service, and do not present its output as describing a real outbreak or a real group of people.

Data you submit

Starting a simulation sends a seed and a set of numeric parameters to the server. No personal information is requested or required. What the system does and does not collect in a real deployment is described in the privacy policy.

Availability and changes

The service is provided as is, with no warranty and no guarantee of availability. Runs are held in memory and are discarded over time. These terms and the underlying system may change without notice.
